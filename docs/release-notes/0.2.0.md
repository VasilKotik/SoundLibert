The first public release of **SoundLibert**, a free, open-source music player for Windows built on the YouTube Music catalogue. It has no ads and no telemetry, and you don't need an account to use it.

## Highlights

- **Always something to find:** Home mixes YouTube Music's picks with songs like the ones you play, new releases from your artists, what's trending, top artists in your country and moods. It changes when you come back and keeps growing as you scroll.
- **The whole catalogue:** songs, albums, artists, playlists and videos. Search opens on moods, genres and charts, and answers with the best match and a shelf of everything it found. Suggestions take you straight to an artist, song or album.
- **Artist pages:** monthly audience, top songs with their plays, the latest release, full discography, official videos, live performances and who else fans like. Every page links to the others, and albums and playlists end with where to go next.
- **Synced lyrics:** lyrics from YouTube Music, timed to the exact recording that plays. LRCLIB is used when YouTube Music has none. They show full-screen or as a single line under the cover.
- **Song / Video switch:** watch the official music video, or keep a muted, looping **Canvas** behind the song. Songs without a Canvas get a moving background made from the colours of the cover. Black bars baked into a video slide out of view, so the picture fills the screen. Scroll below the song for similar songs, the artist, what's up next and related playlists.
- **Videos that keep coming:** **Show all** on an artist's videos keeps loading as you scroll. Home has music videos from the artists you listen to, new music videos and concerts, each with its own page that does the same.
- **Your library:** liked songs, local playlists with cover mosaics, history, offline downloads and your own MP3 / FLAC / WAV / M4A files. Every collection can be filtered and sorted.
- **Optional, read-only sign-in:** import your YouTube Music likes, playlists, albums and subscriptions. SoundLibert never writes anything to your account.
- **A full player:**
  - queue you can reorder by dragging;
  - radio from any track;
  - sleep timer and mini player;
  - crossfade, loudness normalisation and EQ presets;
  - choice of output device.
- **Windows integration:** media keys, the Windows media overlay, playback progress on the taskbar button, and close-to-tray.

## Also in this version

- **A warm welcome:** a short animated opening, then a first screen where you choose a few artists you love, so Home starts from your taste.
- **Automatic updates:** new versions download in the background, and a card shows what is new in them. Choose **Restart now** when you're ready, or it installs the next time you open the app. Settings → Updates has the rest.
- **A calm, living design:** the colours of the cover flow behind the whole window while the music plays. Screens show only what you need, and everything else appears when you reach for it. Reduce motion is one switch away in Settings → Display.
- **Report a bug from the app:** with the bug button at the top of the window, or in Settings → About. You see exactly what will be shared, with IP addresses, emails, link parameters and your Windows user name removed.

## Install

Download **`SoundLibertApp-win-Setup.exe`** below and run it. The installer:

- installs for your user only, with no admin rights needed;
- adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 (1809) or newer, 64-bit. Signing in needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 11 already includes. Windows "N" editions also need Microsoft's Media Feature Pack, or songs cannot play.

> The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.
