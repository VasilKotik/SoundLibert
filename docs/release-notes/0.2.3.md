Likes that keep up, a filter for AI songs, and steadier sound.

- **One like, every version:** YouTube Music lists the same song several times: on the album, on the deluxe edition, as its music video. A like now belongs to the song, so its heart is filled on the album, the artist's page, Liked and in the player alike, and unliking it anywhere clears it everywhere.
- **Album tracks know their artist:** songs started from an album page show the artist in the player and find their lyrics again (they used to read "Pick a track to begin").
- **Plays and views in their place:** songs count plays, videos count views, the same in every list.
- **Don't recommend AI-generated music:** a new switch in Settings → Playback (on by default) keeps known AI-generated artists out of Home, charts, radio and "You might also like", using the community list of [spotify-ai-blocker](https://github.com/CennoxX/spotify-ai-blocker). Search, albums and playlists still show everything, and you can mark any artist yourself from a song's menu.
- **Steadier sound:** a deeper audio buffer, so music is less likely to stutter while you work in another app.
- **Tidier full-screen player:** the window buttons and the player's own sit together on one line; Report a bug moved into the player's ••• menu there.
- **Small things:** the Home refresh button is always visible, downloads and the playing indicator follow a song across its versions too, and a play sign appears when you point at a search suggestion.

## Install

Download **`SoundLibertApp-win-Setup.exe`** below and run it. If you already have SoundLibert, it updates itself: a card appears in the app, and **Restart now** installs the update.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 (1809) or newer, 64-bit. Signing in needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 11 already includes. Windows "N" editions also need Microsoft's Media Feature Pack, or songs cannot play.

> The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.
